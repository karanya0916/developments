Leadership & Experience

Primary Builder: SARGU SANJU

MAMATHA CONSTRUCTIONS & BUILDERS (MCB) was established in Warangal with a clear customer-first mission: to transform the often chaotic residential building journey into a structured, transparent, and joyful experience.

Headquartered at Vishwanath Colony, Shambunipet, Warangal, builder Sargu Sanju brings deep practical civil construction know-how, meticulous attention to material brands, and structural adherence to Indian Standard IS 456. From structural frame calculation to the finishing luster of teakwood woodwork, every project is treated with personal dedication.
